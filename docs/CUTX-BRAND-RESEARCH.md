## What could NOT be verified (hence not in the store)

- Logo/wordmark artwork (profile picture visible but too small to extract reliably; **no clean text invented as "official"** — store uses a generic X-mark + "CUT X" wordmark, documented as interpretive).
- Brand colors, typography, product photography style (login-walled grids; thumbnails not legible).
- Any product name, price, size, color, inventory, material spec, lab result, certification, warranty, shipping fee, delivery time, return policy, phone, address, or email.

## Interpretive choices (marked as assumptions, not facts)

- **Accent color safety red (#E10600)** on near-black: chosen to signal protection/confidence for technical apparel; documented as a design decision, not a brand color.
- **Cairo typeface** retained: supports Arabic + Latin, fits Egyptian market.
- **Categories** (Daily Essentials / Athletic Protection / Security & Workwear / Accessories) derive directly from verified fact #4 — they are research directions confirmed by the bio, populated only with clearly-labelled demo records.
- **Voice**: confident/technical, Egyptian-dialect-friendly Arabic written naturally (not literal translation).
